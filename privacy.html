Privacy Policy

MythosOfTheStars Publisher · Effective 16 October 2026

MythosOfTheStars Publisher ("the App") is a private tool run by the owner of the MythosOfTheStars channels. Its only job is to upload the channel's own original mythology videos to the channel's own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else can sign up for or log in to it.

What the App receives from TikTok

When the account owner connects the MythosOfTheStars TikTok account, TikTok gives the App:

  • an access token and a refresh token, so it can post on the account's behalf;
  • basic account information (the user.info.basic scope): the account's ID, display name and avatar;
  • the account's posting options, such as which privacy levels are available, and the status of each upload.

What the App does not collect

The App collects no information about anyone except the connected account. That includes viewers, followers, commenters and other TikTok users. It uses no cookies, analytics, advertising trackers or profiling.

How the information is used

The App uses the information above for one thing only: to upload videos to the connected account and publish them. It is never sold, rented or shared with anyone.

Where it is stored

Tokens and upload records are stored only on the operator's own computer. They are not sent to any server other than TikTok's.

Keeping and deleting it

Tokens are kept until access is revoked. The account owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok's settings, under the permissions for connected apps. When access is revoked, the stored tokens stop working and are deleted. To ask for deletion, use the contact address below.

Children

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any child's information.

Changes
